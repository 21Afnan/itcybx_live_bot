## Profitable Growth for a Premium Mobile Accessories Brand in Saudi Arabia

How restructuring the ad account and localizing the creative took a volatile 1.37x–2.20x ROAS to 4.29x–4.54x on the rebuilt campaigns.

## 1. The Challenge

A premium mobile accessories brand selling in Saudi Arabia came to us with real demand for its products but an ad account that wasn't turning that demand into profit. Budget was split across many small campaigns, audiences overlapped, clicks were expensive, and results swung from week to week.

During the baseline period in late 2025, the account's campaigns returned between 1.37x and 2.20x ROAS: too low, and too unpredictable, to scale.

## 3. What We Changed

**Consolidated the account.** We cut the overlapping, thinly funded campaigns and moved spend into a small number of campaign-budget-optimized (CBO) campaigns, so Meta had enough data in each one to find buyers consistently.

**Localized the creative.** We replaced generic ads with hooks written for Saudi buyers: fast delivery in Riyadh and Jeddah, product durability, and premium design. Cost per click fell to as low as SAR 0.25.

**Optimized for purchases, not clicks.** We set the campaigns to optimize for completed purchases instead of traffic. The two rebuilt campaigns delivered 109 orders.

## 5. The Result

Once the account was simplified and the ads spoke to Saudi buyers directly, the same products sold profitably at 4.29x–4.54x ROAS. The lesson for GCC brands: structure first, then scale. Running more tests on a fragmented account rarely fixes the problem.

Results reflect this client's account during the period shown. Outcomes depend on product, market and budget; past results don't guarantee future performance.
