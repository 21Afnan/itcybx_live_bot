## 1. The Challenge

An online fashion-jewelry store in Pakistan came to us losing money on ads. Its budget was spread thin across many separate campaigns (catalog sales, video views and general engagement), which starved Meta's pixel of the data it needs to learn who buys.

Before we stepped in, catalog tests and seasonal video campaigns were returning as little as 0.12x–0.24x ROAS, and a single purchase could cost Rs 4,856.64. Overlapping audiences and scattered tracking meant most of the spend was wasted, and the store was losing money overall.

## 3. What We Changed

**Consolidated the account.** We paused the fragmented campaigns and moved the budget into one campaign-budget-optimized (CBO) campaign, putting Rs 266,103.78 of spend behind a single, well-fed campaign so Meta could learn quickly.

**Rewrote the ads.** Weak copy had pushed cost per click to between Rs 21.38 and Rs 29.37. New hooks built around current fashion trends in Pakistan brought the average CPC down to Rs 8.24.

**Cut the cost per sale.** With delivery stable, cost per purchase fell from a peak of Rs 4,856.64 to Rs 508.80, making sales profitable.

## 5. The Result

For a jewelry brand in Pakistan, growth came from concentrating data, not multiplying campaigns. One consolidated campaign turned a loss-making ad budget into a reliable, profitable source of sales, and a template for scaling from here.

Results reflect this client's account during the period shown. Outcomes depend on product, market and budget; past results don't guarantee future performance.
