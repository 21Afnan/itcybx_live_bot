This policy explains how itcybx.co.uk uses cookies and similar technologies.
3.1 What cookies are
Cookies are small text files stored on your device that help a website function, remember preferences, and understand usage.
3.2 Types of cookies we use

Strictly necessary — required for the Site to work (security, basic functionality). These cannot be switched off.
Analytics/performance — help us understand how visitors use the Site (e.g. [Google Analytics]). Used only with your consent.
Marketing/advertising — measure and improve advertising (e.g. [Meta Pixel]). Used only with your consent.
Functional — remember your preferences (e.g. language).

3.3 Consent
When you first visit, our cookie banner lets you accept or reject non-essential cookies. You can change your choice at any time via [cookie settings link / your browser]. Strictly necessary cookies do not require consent.
3.4 Managing cookies
You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings. Blocking some cookies may affect how the Site works.
3.5 Third-party cookies
Some cookies are set by third parties (e.g. analytics and advertising providers). Their use of data is governed by their own privacy policies.
3.6 Changes & contact
We may update this policy. Questions: info@itcybx.co.uk.
