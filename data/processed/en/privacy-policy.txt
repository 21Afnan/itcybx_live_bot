Information you give us: when you submit a form, book an audit, or contact us — your name, email address, phone/WhatsApp number, store URL, company name, and any message content.

To respond to enquiries and provide quotes.
To deliver, manage, and improve our services.
To process payments and send invoices/receipts.
To send service-related communications.
To send marketing communications where you have consented (you can opt out anytime).
To comply with legal obligations and protect our rights.

performance of a contract (to deliver services you request); consent (marketing, non-essential cookies); legitimate interests (to run and improve our business, where not overridden by your rights); and legal obligation (tax, accounting).

We keep personal data only as long as necessary for the purposes above, or as required by law (e.g. tax records). We delete or anonymize it when no longer needed.
