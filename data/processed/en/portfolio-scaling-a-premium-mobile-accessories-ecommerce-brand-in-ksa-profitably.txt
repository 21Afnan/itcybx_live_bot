The iT CYBX Core Growth Architecture

Account Consolidation & Liquidity Overhaul: We eliminated budget dilution across minor, overlapping audience structures. Moving away from highly fragmented ad configurations, we centralized total data signals directly into algorithmic Campaign Budget Optimization (CBO) sets to secure consistent conversion paths.
Localized Hook & Creative Optimization: Replaced generalized creatives with target, high-fidelity native hooks focusing on KSA consumer behaviors, highlighting Riyadh/Jeddah premium logistics, accessory durability, and sleek tech designs. This triggered a dramatic drop in CPC down to 0.25 ر.س.
Conversion Value & Margin Alignment: Instead of optimizing purely for generic top-of-funnel traffic clicks, we anchored optimization on down-funnel purchase conversions, driving 109 cumulative high-value orders across our scaling frameworks.
